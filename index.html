This app contains home and away fixture data for the 2026 season. Use Game week schedule to see who plays where each week — switch between Home (field grid) and Away (venue list). Use Team comparison to track up to 4 teams across all games. The Field usage report counts home games per field. Fixtures after 28 Jun are unannounced and hidden by default — use the toggle at the top to reveal them.
